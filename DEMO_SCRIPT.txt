               PROSPECTOR AI - DEMO SCRIPT
              "4 Hours of Research in 60 Seconds"
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎯 OBJECTIVE
Demonstrate how Prospector AI automates B2B prospect research using AI

⏱️ DURATION: 2-3 minutes

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                      THE SCRIPT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[OPENING - 10 seconds]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"Let me show you Prospector AI. It's an AI tool that does 4 
hours of prospect research in just 60 seconds. Watch this..."

[SCREEN: Show Streamlit UI at localhost:8501]


[STEP 1: ENTER URL - 5 seconds]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Type: chat360.io

"I just enter any company URL - this is Chat360, an Indian 
conversational AI platform..."


[STEP 2: START RESEARCH - 5 seconds]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Click "🔍 Research" button

"Now it's scraping their website, careers page, searching for 
recent news, and analyzing everything with GPT-4..."

[WHILE PROCESSING - 20 seconds]
"What would normally take a sales rep 2-4 hours of manual 
research - reading their website, checking LinkedIn, looking 
at job postings - Prospector AI does automatically. Let's see 
what it finds..."


[STEP 3: SHOW RESULTS - 90 seconds]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

► TAB 1: SCORE BREAKDOWN [20 seconds]
"Look at this - it calculated an intent score of 72 out of 100.
That's HIGH PRIORITY.

Here's why:
- 20/25 points for hiring signals - they're actively hiring 
  for sales roles, which means they're scaling
- 15/20 for funding - they're backed and have budget
- They have active news coverage

So we know this is a hot lead worth pursuing."


► TAB 2: IDENTIFIED GAPS [30 seconds]
"But here's the really powerful part - the AI identified their 
PRIMARY GAP.

[Read the gap title and description]

'Enterprise credibility gap - they target big enterprise clients 
like Ford and Honda, but they have almost zero G2 reviews. 
Enterprise buyers need third-party validation during 
procurement.'

This is ACTIONABLE INTELLIGENCE. I know exactly what angle to 
use in my outreach - I can talk about helping them build 
credibility with enterprise buyers."


► TAB 3: OUTREACH MESSAGES [30 seconds]
"And it doesn't stop there. It wrote 3 personalized outreach 
messages I can use RIGHT NOW.

[Show gap-based message]

Look at this one - it references the specific gap we just saw.
It's not generic. It's personalized. It's relevant.

I can literally copy this and send it today."


► TAB 4: SOURCES [10 seconds]
"And everything is fully cited with sources, so I can verify 
every claim if needed."


[CLOSING - 20 seconds]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"So in 60 seconds, I got:
✓ An intent score with detailed breakdown
✓ Strategic gaps identified by AI
✓ 3 ready-to-send personalized messages
✓ Full source citations

This would have taken me 3-4 hours to research manually.

And the best part? It's OPEN SOURCE. You can self-host it, 
customize it, and it costs about 2 cents per company in API 
fees."


[OPTIONAL: SHOW API]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
If technical audience, open: localhost:8000/docs

"It also has a full REST API, so you can integrate this into 
your existing sales stack, CRM, or build automation on top of it."
